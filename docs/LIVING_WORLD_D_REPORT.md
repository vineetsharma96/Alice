# Living-world D report — 20 October 2026

D implements bounded native animal care and food production. The physical acceptance checks pass within the scope below; **reliable autonomous husbandry, breeding and cooking are not completed**. The failed Phase 9 study remains unchanged and its learner is excluded from live policies. No neural training, speech or true 3D mechanics were added. All measured runs use zero model calls; A's recurring/fair/global model-budget gate remains incomplete.

## Implementation and persistence

`core/ecology.cpp` is authoritative for a fictional mossgrazer and reedhen, finite forage, explicit regeneration inputs, age, health/needs/reserves, bounded local behavior, human care and egg production. Native observe_animal, offer_feed, guide_or_lure, collect_eggs and gather_plant use the same strict envelopes, actor scheduling, visibility, reach, collision, stock/capacity and delayed terminal revalidation as existing actions. C fill_trough transfers real water; animals consume actual trough contents. Animals can seek forage/water, rest, avoid unfamiliar people, follow a familiar caretaker briefly, and wander. No per-animal model calls occur.

Structural animal mass is initialized explicitly and retained, including dead bodies. Food reserve metabolism and egg production transfer into exact integer egg/residue quantities, water into retained waste. Forage regeneration is a named external biomass source, default zero, capped to the patch's 2 kg capacity. It is not nutrient cycling or photosynthesis. Food reserves abstract dietary energy; no chemical or metabolic heat conversion is claimed. Thermal accounting is unchanged, and no heat/fuel-coupled cook_food action exists.

Each 0.08 kg egg-production debit yields 0.06 kg eggs and 0.02 kg retained residue. Production is conditional on actual reserve/condition/age and limited by accumulated stock; time alone grants no food. New gathered plant food lasts 120 simulated seconds, eggs 180. Holder/material freshness mixes by minimum, slows to half rate inside boxes and never refreshes on collection or transfer. Spoilage retains exact mass as non-edible residue. Historical abstract food remains nonperishable. Optional eat material selects eggs/plant_food; its historical food default remains available. No detailed nutritional quality or microbiology is modeled.

Familiarity is per individual and caretaker, credited only by successful feeding receipts, at most once per ten simulated seconds, capped at five. Three credits change that individual's handling state to domestic/habituated; guide_or_lure requires two credits and expires after 20 simulated seconds. Urgent needs override following. These are authored handling rules, not genetic/neural learning. Private care histories and exact reserves remain in operator diagnostics; agent observations expose local appearance/condition and a sourced observation report with only that observer's familiarity.

Package **0.11.0**, native **UW9**, SQLite **DB10**, checkpoint **9**, controller **stabilization/3**. Native build ID: `832c4a705a7ceecd85d8befb3d84f4dff0d4730f024c395eba12985f0d8bd896`. Ecology is opt-in via config/living-world-d.toml and initialized only in a NEW world. No old world is loaded/reset/replenished or silently migrated. C's binary/data/evidence remain retained; older saves need their original complete pinned runtime. This checkout intentionally rejects older formats.

## Remaining limitations and operation

Breeding, gestation/incubation/newborn mass conversion and fuel/heat-coupled cooking are deferred. There is no disease/predation/carcass conversion, lot-level food aging, validated nutrition/thermal metabolism, sustainable ecosystem or long-term population claim. Familiarity is authored; practice counters from C remain separate. Human care is assigned, not automatically part of the stabilization survival controller; generalized planning is E, knowledge workflows F, integrated demonstration G. Animals can get stuck locally; bounded sensing does not reveal hidden resources or solve arbitrary maze navigation. C's circular solids approximate a pen rather than continuous wall polygons. Waste is an aggregate retained reservoir, not a rendered/manipulable manure object. Longer multi-seed durable survival and sustained eight-person operation remain unverified.

Build/launch commands and the saved compatible world are in [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md). Use run to continue an existing save; init is explicitly NEW and refuses overwrite. No push is performed by this milestone request.
