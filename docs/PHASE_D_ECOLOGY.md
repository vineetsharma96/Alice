## Quantities and accelerated rules

Mass is integer micrograms, 1 kg = 1e9 ug. Two adults are initialized: fictional mossgrazer (2 kg structural body) and reedhen (0.8 kg structural body). Structural body mass stays in a separate closed ledger, including dead bodies; no reproduction or carcass conversion. Each starts with 0.25 kg food reserve and 0.20 kg water reserve. Initial forage is 2 kg, with a 2 kg cap. New-world initial reserves and forage are declared initialization inputs, never episode refills.

Forage regeneration is an explicitly configured external biomass boundary input, default zero, at most 0.01 kg/s. It is not photosynthesis or nutrient conservation. Each animal burns up to 0.001 kg food and 0.0015 kg water per simulated second into retained food residue and water waste. Food reserve cap is 0.4 kg; water cap is 0.3 kg. Needs derive from reserves; lack of food/water decreases condition at 0.01/s, while adequately supplied animals recover at 0.002/s. Age advances continuously; adulthood starts at 60 simulated seconds (initial age 120). All rates are fictional accelerated abstractions.

The reedhen may lay after 30 seconds when healthy and adequately supplied: 0.08 kg reserve becomes 0.06 kg eggs plus 0.02 kg food residue. At most 0.30 kg eggs can accumulate. Production requires actual reserve; elapsed time alone cannot create food. An explicit production counter and food-family balance reconcile eggs, reserves, waste, forage, carried food and ingestion. Structural animal mass and water have separate closure checks.

Animals sense within 4 m with line of sight. They seek forage/water, rest, avoid unfamiliar nearby humans, follow a familiar handler briefly, or wander. Movement is at most 0.4 m/s and uses the shared swept-circle collision rules and region bounds. No ownership flag confines an animal. Dead bodies remain solid. No animal LLM calls or learned-policy claim.

## Human actions and food

`observe_animal` provides a local source-linked condition report, not other actors' private histories. `offer_feed` transfers a positive exact food mass from the actor to an accessible animal, within reserve capacity. `fill_trough` retains C's real carried-water transfer. `guide_or_lure` requires at least two successful feeding interactions and expires after 20 seconds; urgent needs override it. Familiarity increases at most once per 10 simulated seconds from successful feed receipts, caps at five, and after three recorded feed interactions the individual becomes habituated/domestic. It is a bounded authored handling rule, not instant obedience or neural learning. `collect_eggs` transfers existing eggs only. `gather_plant` converts accessible finite forage into carried plant food. Every delayed action revalidates life, visibility, reach, stock and carrying/reserve capacity at completion. Idempotent request receipts prevent duplicate effects.

New plant food expires after 120 seconds, eggs after 180. Per-holder/material remaining freshness uses conservative minimum on mixing; storage boxes halve elapsed aging, never refresh food. Expiration transfers the exact mass into non-edible food residue. Existing abstract `food` retains its historical nonperishable semantics and is labeled as such. `eat` may explicitly select plant_food or eggs, preserving the historical food default. Animal feeding may use those materials as an abstract equal-quality food. Animal eggs age before collection. Cooking and breeding are deferred until this production gate is measured; existing thermal vessels do not imply a validated cooking coupling.

## Acceptance protocol

Development: one-person 16 m seed 21 and controlled native fixtures. Held-out: seeds 44 and 73, different food/water availability and obstacle layouts. Fixtures must declare all initial inputs and preserve ledgers; diagnostic checkpoint editing is test-only, never a live controller input.

Evaluation refinement before final measurement: the exploratory 44/73 fixtures translated the same layout and produced the same trajectories. They are development checks, not evidence of layout generalization. Freeze native rules and the bounded local care route, then use new held-out seeds 101/202 with swapped human/animal/trough positions and an additional interior stone obstacle (relocated existing stock). Both treatment and control begin with identical food/water carried by the human, debited from existing stocks. Only the treatment executes real offer_feed/fill_trough actions. The control does nothing; no resources are deleted between cases. Measure 30/120/300 continuous seconds, all requests including failures, reserve-funded production, health and exact replay from 120 seconds. This validates bounded care consequences, not a learned planner. Retain the exploratory evidence separately.

Native-behavior amendment before the final rerun: the first four-person candidate showed no animal forage intake by 120 seconds despite nearby forage. Investigation found a shared enter/leave threshold caused repeated small sips and short abandoned forage approaches. Add hysteresis: enter water seeking below 0.15 kg, continue until 0.25 kg; enter forage seeking below 0.20 kg, continue until 0.35 kg. Capacities remain 0.30/0.40 kg; no resource is granted. Require actual food and water consumption with both accessible through 300 seconds in development. Preserve the superseded candidate artifacts. Freeze the human care route and native fix before new held-out layouts 303/404, which change actor/animal positions and interior stone placement. Previous 101/202 results are development evidence after this investigation, not untouched confirmation. Keep the same outcome thresholds and disclose failures rather than tuning after final held-out results.

Require zero overdrafts and exact integer food/water/body balances; no duplicate eggs on replay or competing collection; expiration cannot refresh through transfer; native schema, hidden/out-of-reach, stale/depleted targets and capacity failures preserve stock. Unfed animals must lose condition within 300 continuous seconds; supplied animals must show higher health and actual recorded consumption. At least one reserve-funded egg must be laid, collected and eaten. A closed physical pen must contain an animal through 120 seconds of actual movement attempts; opening its gate must permit a measured exit, with no teleport. In-flight care, production clocks, age, familiarity, food freshness, waste, resources, request receipts and scheduler must match save/resume exactly. Run progressively longer 30/120/300-second continuous native scenarios and a SQLite scheduler scenario as compute permits; report simulated/wall duration separately. Existing native/Python regressions must pass. Keep all C and sealed Phase 9 evidence unchanged. No claim of long-term ecosystem sustainability from these bounded checks.
