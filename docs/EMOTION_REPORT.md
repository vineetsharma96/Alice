## Failed development and remaining stages

Development2's behavior gate **FAILED**:9.9C was rounded by native senses to10C,
so alleged safe reversal trained a different cue. Corrected apparatus9.4C gives
perceived9C. Learning equations and acceptance thresholds were unchanged; passing
development3 preceded untouched confirmation. Initial result-export path and
JSON retry-shape defects were corrected before confirmation. Failed outputs,
saves and matching source archives remain. See [development notes](EMOTION_DEVELOPMENT_NOTES.md).

F4-F12 remain **unvalidated and deferred at this first milestone boundary**.
Slow stress, episode links and empty relationship/regulation fields are state
plumbing, not completed temperament, salience, attachment, social inference,
regulation, emotional symbols, morality, identity or lifelong gates. Valence and
stress are currently diagnostic; the demonstrated causal decision contribution
comes from learned personal cue expectation. General affect-driven attention,
exploration, help-seeking and persistence remain future work.

Next is a separately registered F4 experiment: declared initial temperament,
long exposure followed by real supplied safety, immediate-versus-slow recovery,
multiple seeds and long-run nonsaturation. Advance to F5 only after that passes.
Do not relabel existing trajectories as a prospective F4 study. H-learning,
private communication and weather still require an explicit combined-source/save
compatibility design before claiming F12 integration. Coarse weather classes can
overgeneralize; no arbitrary animal fear, lethal-danger calibration, learned
social intention or general intelligence is established.
