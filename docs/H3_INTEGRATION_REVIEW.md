## Native replacement

The preregistered replacement is
`docs/DEVELOPMENTAL_H3_NATIVE_PROTOCOL.md`. Its source registration is
`artifacts/h3_native_registration.json`. `underworld/community.py` keeps private
partner-specific counts over actual native grounding proofs, permits one declared
activation of a never-trained cohort member, and bounds active and archived
memory. Excess protected entries or archive capacity stop explicitly. An audit
checks that each private association's feature, speaker and token match its native
grounding evidence, not merely that some record has a valid checksum.

All teaching is authored. The listener's tie detector may request an authored
physical demonstration; no learned repair grammar or autonomous teaching is
claimed. Reference scoring after repair uses new untouched objects and a new
speech action, removing the direct physical deposit cue. Listener interpretation
receives neither the intended target nor another person's private vocabulary.
Actual native movement, time, hearing and inspection validators remain intact.

Each complete scored arm resumes a labelled counterfactual SQLite backup in a
fresh process. The five branches start at one matched native head, retain bodies,
needs, resources, memories and scheduler state, and are never represented as one
continuous life. Twelve native interference exposures compete under a six-slot
active budget; the three consolidated core associations are protected by an
explicit authored rule. Nine weak associations move to a bounded archive with
native evidence. This does not show that retention protection was itself learned.

## Limits

The new native H3 study is narrow, short and scaffolded. Its confirmation seeds
are explicitly reused seed numbers from earlier milestones in new cohorts;
they are held out from this H3 development, not globally novel. It does not test
homonym repair, autonomous teaching, spontaneous goals, independent survival,
long unattended operation or meaningful resource-location cooperation. H4/H5,
neural training, reproduction and true 3D are outside this change. No new
application/release label is assigned. The existing executable remains G.

Native study audits require the local saved worlds; absence is an error, not
proof of zero model use. Compressed report traces alone cannot verify a missing
live database. Normal audits assume a quiescent research controller; multiwriter
H3 coordination is not established. The retained H1 online-adaptation counterexample
and older G husbandry failures retain their recorded status.

The user's existing save was checked read-only at tick 10765 / 5382.5 simulated
seconds and replay matched. This turn did not advance or reset that save; it does
not establish that its previous continuous-run process is still operating.
