## What the native environment means

Climate v1 has clear, cloudy and rain states; cold is a temperature condition.
An independent seeded finite transition model persists from/current/target
conditions, transition progress, next schedule and PRNG. Authored ranges bound
rain to 0-12 mm/hour, cloud fraction to 0-1 and wind components to ±10 m/s.
All calendar time derives from committed native ticks, never browser or wall time.

The fictional spring/summer/autumn/winter anchors are 12/24/11/0 °C, with smooth
temperature, rainfall-probability, daylight and ecological-growth envelopes.
Research uses 600 seconds per season, 2400 per year. The normal profile uses
31557600 seconds per year. Neither changes the existing 86400-second solar day,
dt, body rates, tool wear or animal clocks. Actual native sunlight is the old
daylight wave times season amplitude and cloud attenuation; it enters the existing
energy ledger. Weather air temperature does not turn the four-vessel laboratory
into a whole-world atmosphere or add unimplemented body heat exchanges.

Wetness and cold burden are bounded dimensionless proxies. Native rain, wind,
temperature, shelter condition and actual position affect them each tick. They
alter fatigue and sustained severe exposure causes direct health loss. Their
cumulative ledgers are distinct from ordinary needs damage and ordinary recovery;
direct environmental loss is not the same quantity as net health decline. No
rain water mass, thermoregulation reservoir or closed water cycle is claimed.

Seasonal growth multiplies only configured native tree/forage boundary rates,
retaining capacity and remainder accounting. In each 100-second growth fixture,
tree boundary input was 0.099492591 kg and forage food input 0.099488887 kg;
both zero-rate controls remained exactly zero. The auditor recomputed these
native fixtures from initialization. Winter does not delete standing wood or
transform food. The long life uses zero regeneration.

## Physical shelters and actual senses

The fixed open lean-to costs 0.8 kg wood + 0.1 kg fiber, with 12 seconds of
native work after a one-second site transfer. Existing branch harvest of 1 kg
tree biomass produces 0.9 kg wood and 0.1 kg fiber, leaving 0.1 kg wood after
the roof. This fits the 2 kg carrying limit and the region's finite 18 kg initial
tree biomass. Material transfers, delayed revalidation, reservations, native work
and stable request IDs reuse existing construction. Interrupted work persists;
incomplete sites provide no roof protection. There is no free task completion.

A completed roof covers a 1.1 m radius, with an illustrative 2 m height. Body
radius must fit inside coverage; nearby roofs provide no remote benefit. Up to
four physically separated inhabitants are protected, with deterministic overflow
assignment; the fifth-body fixture receives no protection. The roof interior is
open, with no solid wall disc or closing door. Rendered posts are decorative.
At full condition, rain exposure factor is 0.1, wind 0.5 and cold drive 0.7.
These are authored coefficients, not measured shelter performance. Ordinary
condition wear is 0.00002/s; repair consumes 0.1 kg real wood and three seconds
of native work, retaining the wood in structure composition and restoring up
to 0.2 condition. Destructive storms are absent.

Own observations include rounded felt temperature, visible rain/cloud class,
noticed wind, own wetness/cold and locally observed shelters. They exclude the
exact weather target/schedule/PRNG, hidden shelters and other people's private
state. Private coordinates originate from actual own observations; foreign/future
memory is refused. Routing, novelty, needs and construction rules are authored.
Operator-assigned execution and authored autonomous decisions have distinct
origin labels. No developmental weather integration or language learning is claimed.

The same four-person development life starts with finite food 8 kg and water
16 kg, zero regeneration and native bodies. Physically executed assigned setup
harvests materials, builds a starting roof and positions inhabitants, ending at
134 simulated seconds. The continuing controller uses only their own senses.
No weather forcing, resource reset, material injection or body reset occurs.

Every window used a fresh process and the same saved world, preserving memories,

retains the complete journal from initialization; no epoch/history rotation was
used. Total continuation cost was 481.164 wall seconds, 486.075 including setup.
The final calendar is spring, year index 1: all four phase boundaries were crossed
without starting a new world. The 86400-second day was not accelerated.

All four finish alive at health 1. Hunger is approximately 0.45/0.45/0.55/0.45

There were 91 actual eating and 140 drinking actions, 230 completed moves over

markers. The zero-progress metric is only a narrow wasted-movement proxy; it
does not establish every trip was necessary. There were 1509 completed policy

The natural year added about 0.240-0.259 cumulative environmental fatigue per
inhabitant, but no direct environmental health loss in this particular life.
That result alone does not prove shelter was necessary or demonstrate superiority
over a matched full-year legacy control. Causal protection evidence comes from
the separate matched severe-weather cases. This is one finite scaffolded year,
not multi-seed long survival, animal care or indefinite operation.

## Failures, checks and cost

All failed development evidence remains separate and unchanged:

1. Initial clock/storage fixtures had list/tuple normalization, trailing checkpoint
   whitespace and an unclosed test SQLite connection. Corrected fixtures passed;
   native acceptance semantics were not weakened.
2. A genuine forcing boundary bug applied exposure for one tick too few. The
   retained five-second rain test measured wetness 0.056 instead of 0.060.
   Native interval accounting was corrected; the unchanged threshold passes.
3. Browser testing found a missing `three.core.min.js` route. The route was added;
   a separate resize test now waits for responsive layout to settle. Final Edge
   desktop/mobile rendering and no-overflow/error checks pass.
4. Development 1 failed sharing with only two people ever protected. Development
   2 and 3 reached all four individually but never simultaneously. Routine half-full
   needs and long serial rest frames caused goal churn/congestion. Before any
   held-out measurement, authored trip hysteresis, 0.6 routine/0.8 urgent thresholds,
   a 30-second settling interval and one-second protected-rest frames fixed it.
   Body rates and the four-person gate were unchanged. Development 4/5 and a
   dedicated four-person regression pass. Exact candidate source ZIPs and logs
   retain the failures; no held-out tuning or retrospective gate weakening.
5. The supplementary continuity auditor initially assumed a synthetic UW9 adapter
   `interventions` field existed in raw native snapshots. Its corrected audit
   uses the new journal/forcing/material authorities. Simulation data and primary
   frozen sources were not changed; the failed audit log is retained.

Final verification: 28 weather checks pass; original 335 Python tests pass in
599.344 seconds (driver wall 600.699), plus four native checks. Confirmation
took 127.614 wall seconds, with seed times 46.177/38.991/42.424. The weather arm
completed 116 actions per reachable-shelter case versus 30 in the primary survival
control; one-second protected rests increase computation. Weather arm wall times
were 8.962-13.453 seconds versus primary control 1.413-2.615 and exact legacy
utility 0.729-0.759. Some runs overlapped; these are elapsed observations, not
isolated CPU benchmarks or a performance superiority claim. No model calls.

The final observer passes real headless Edge 1440x900 and 390x844 checks: actual
3D draw calls, agent selection, intervention label, stable paused native hash,
read-only refusal, no page errors and no horizontal overflow. Screenshots were

## Remaining limits

Weather transitions, proxies, rates, protection coefficients, blueprint, routing
and priorities are authored. No learned weather anticipation, autonomous shelter
discovery, developmental weather integration, shared dictionary, new speech,
emotion, reproduction, neural training or true 3D physics was added. There is
no consciousness/general-intelligence/civilization claim.

Rain collection, snow/floods/lightning/wildfire/destructive storms, enclosed wall/
door thermodynamics, realistic body heat/water reservoirs and closed ecological
cycles remain deferred. Repair has direct native/regression evidence, but was not
needed during the full year. Ecological coupling has native configured-fixture
evidence; the ordinary weather CLI does not initialize animal care. Global forest
exhaustion, every blocked-path topology, unknown-material bootstrap, multi-seed
year-long survival and indefinite memory/history retention remain unproven.
Finite quotas refuse explicitly. The old research failures remain failures.
