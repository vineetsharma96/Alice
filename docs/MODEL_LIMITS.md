## Planned physical approximations

Fixed-step coarse classical motion; circular collision geometry; finite sensing and simple occlusion. No quantum mechanics, relativity, molecular dynamics, full fluid dynamics, weather, geophysics or Earth-scale simulation. Daylight will be an authored boundary energy source. Materials will use aggregate species masses and bulk thermal reservoirs with tested numerical tolerances; no chemistry exists now. Precision/determinism guarantees will be limited to the same supported runtime/build.

## Planned biological approximations

Authored scalar hunger, thirst, fatigue, health and simple costs/recovery/death rules. Food and drinking will change abstract needs and accounted material reservoirs according to declared rules, not simulate digestion. No metabolism, cells, neural development, endocrine systems, detailed disease, biochemistry, realistic reproduction or aging. Future generations may be abstract cohort replacement. These limitations must accompany any survival results.

## Planned mental approximations

Pretrained language reasoning is an external prior; prompts cannot prove ignorance. Model text, bounded planning, separate episodic records, belief confidence, lexical retrieval and authored utility policies approximate agency. No conscious experience, sentience, human equivalence, unlimited progress or training from memory accumulation is claimed. Shared weights must not imply shared private context. Relationships require observed events. Authored skills/recipes must be labelled separately from discovery and transfer. Randomized novel mechanisms plus controls are needed to test knowledge transfer.

## Feasibility boundaries

CPU-only model-off scaffold is the baseline. No local model is selected/downloaded, no inference RAM/VRAM is measured, and no survival or simulation-throughput claim is supported. A 4 GiB host may be too constrained for useful simultaneous inference; any later adapter needs memory/latency measurement and graceful model-off fallback. A faster pretrained model is an external upgrade. A static scene does not demonstrate time, persistence, autonomous behaviour or acceleration.

## Phase 9 learning limits

Phase 9 adds a three-weight non-neural policy trained on one authored inventory-feasibility task. Parameters persist across fresh short episodes, not one uninterrupted evolving world. Eat and drink share an inventory precondition, so retention after the second block is a narrow interference check; it cannot establish general resistance to catastrophic forgetting. Five training-order seeds and one fixed evaluation set provide descriptive uncertainty only. The authored environment, task choice, learning algorithm, thresholds and memory controls are external engineering priors. A rejected action can be valid negative feedback; a crash, foreign receipt or unrelated rejection cannot.

Native trial receipts establish physical action outcomes, not truth of arbitrary text or books. The learner receives only its own inventory/request features and native outcome labels; no privileged viewer diagnostics, prompts, pretrained weights or cross-agent private memories enter its policy. Its parameter journal is not cryptographic authentication against an adversary controlling local files. Evaluation probe outcomes are privileged experiment labels, explicitly withheld from prediction and updates.

No neural dependency, inherited population, reproduction, selection, artificial-life origins track, consciousness measure, or guarantee of civilization emergence is implemented. Advancement requires benefit over both memory controls plus retention/integrity checks. A completed negative experiment keeps the learner outside autonomous execution. Longer multi-seed world horizons and true 3D mechanics remain deferred.
