## Implemented behavior

Each person has persistent, authored initial sensitivity, mood rise/recovery
constants and baseline valence. Balanced and sensitive parameters were evaluated
in matched native scenarios. A calm preset and bounded custom parameters also
exist; their entire parameter range has not received the same native confirmation.
Temperament does not learn or change during this milestone.

One slow stress component tracks body-derived discomfort above a declared comfort
allowance. Its mood valence derives from that component, while fast affect and
immediate episodes remain distinct. It uses simulated seconds, finite ranges and
exact exponential constant-input integration. No random mood, wall-clock decay,
free stress clearing, inferred intention or invented emotional vocabulary.

F4 replaces the old integrator through checked hooks into our frozen source in a
separate namespace. It does not execute two competing mood engines or maintain a
second copy of the full controller. Original44 sources stay byte-identical;
the derived profile has its own schema, source pins, IDs and transactional tables.
Attachment modifies only a NEW backup copy and retains inherited cue learning,
stress, episodes, factual links, native pending actions and scheduler position.

An authored bounded mood contribution can ease the existing learned-cue caution
threshold from -.12 toward -.08. It still requires a personally learned negative
cue, confidence, an otherwise idle baseline and a feasible locally observed roof.
Native validation, urgent needs and retry cooldown remain authoritative. A
disconnected-mood control retains mood but uses the old threshold. A seeded
cognitive software fixture produces a completed native movement under connected
mood and no additional movement when disconnected. **This fixture is not evidence
that the cue or regulation strategy was learned naturally.**

## Limits and continuation

The core is a one-dimensional slow stress model with derived valence, authored
initial traits and a bounded authored decision rule. It does not establish trait
learning, general mood-driven attention/exploration, learned regulation, social
emotions or a natural survival advantage. Recovery gates concern the declared
presets and controlled supplied conditions. A hungry or exposed person may
appropriately retain stress. Longer or indefinite natural horizons remain unproven.

F5 and later are not implemented or validated here. The next authorized milestone
would need prospective salience/encoding/retrieval and correction tests without
rewriting factual episodes. The H-learning/private communication and weather
profiles still require explicit combined-source/save integration before F12 can
be claimed. Existing successes and failures remain unchanged.
