disabled-equivalence regressions must pass. Disabled policy requests, receipts,

F3: five independent held-out worlds. Explicitly guided, journaled cold/rain
exposure provides harm experience; real rest under warm weather provides safety.
Construct a roof using native ingredients/work BEFORE enrollment; this is assigned
setup, never learned construction. Reuse personal associations across perceived
locations within the declared cue class, never across people or world identities.

learning-frozen, disconnected and disabled arms. At least 4/5 learned arms must
choose a feasible safety movement while frozen/disconnected/disabled do not;
all arms' first requests must complete without rejection. Probe rain with mild
temperature as a superficially similar harmless control: zero learned avoidance.
After safe experience under the original cue class, at least 4/5 must stop
avoiding. Native evidence, confidence and changes must survive exact restart.
