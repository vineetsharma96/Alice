## What learns, and what is authored

The novice profile begins with no outcome table, validated procedure, invented
meaning or partner vocabulary. Native sensors/categories, mass/range bins, motor
actions, body laws, PRNG, count update, task curriculum and communication alphabet
are authored priors. Recipes remain existing authored physical laws. Native
observations/results are retained as experiences; count associations change
later action selection. No neural training, LLM or Phase9 update code is used.

H1 stores reusable sensory/outcome counts separately from object-specific memory.
Its `(successes+1)/(n+2)` mean is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability.
Policies access their own indexed state/evidence only. Native rejections preserve
their actual categories. Goal success requires a completed gather and observable
food gain; accepted requests and completed non-food gathers do not earn credit.

H2 starts each person's production/comprehension empty. A speaker coins unused
one-token IDs with its own saved randomness. A listener learns partner-specific
associations from its own native visible mass change plus a newly delivered
native token. No vocabulary is copied or synchronized. The16-token alphabet is
a channel constraint, not a dictionary. Native speech payloads contain no target
ID or gloss. The privileged oracle comparator is separate from every learner;
no designed shared dictionary was inserted even for that upper comparator.

## H2 confirmation

[H2 protocol](DEVELOPMENTAL_H2_PROTOCOL.md) and
[source registration](../artifacts/developmental_h2_registration.json) were frozen
before confirmation. The same five seed numbers define separately named two-person
research cohorts. No H1/mature person is erased or reinitialized as a new learner.

Training uses36 balanced, shuffled native deposit/speech demonstrations over
three authored sensory classes: small food, larger food and small water. The
visible deposit is explicit teaching support. Speaker supplies are actually
gathered; native transfer, time, hearing and occlusion decide effects/delivery.
Counts are learned from the listener's own observations, never a speaker-target
lookup or fabricated public feedback. Demonstrations are authored teaching,
not spontaneous communication decisions.

Each trained life has five explicitly labelled counterfactual branches, with
matched initial native/private states. Each arm uses12 balanced new-ID/shuffled
scene trials. No deposit, gesture or target-revealing movement occurs in scoring.
The receiver sees its own alternatives and actual token only, then performs a
native inspection. The entire learned scored arm runs after fresh-process resume;
both vocabulary hashes remain unchanged in evaluation.

## Persistence, compatibility and remaining limits

Pending native intents precede execution; reconciliation consumes own terminal
receipts once and commits experience/count updates atomically. Private RNG,
identity, source observations and vocabulary persist through SQLite backup and
restart. Protected native proof survives archival beyond recent-episode lookup.
The developmental archive wrapper checks all private tables before pruning,
rejects unsafe intents/stale learner snapshots and refuses source-bearing epoch
eviction. It stops on finite quotas rather than silently forgetting. Use this
wrapper for learner saves, not the generic G-only archive fingerprint.

No existing G/F world is migrated or repurposed. Original G source/native pins
and its executable remain valid. Old pre-repair research saves require their
preserved exact Python/config sources with the unchanged G native build. The
current desktop executable is still G; the new research path is source CLI only.
No new observer panel was added, so no changed-panel browser claim is made.

This demonstrates a small count learner and a scaffolded one-token naming
protocol over known sensory classes. It does not establish arbitrary object
names, locations, grammar, compositionality, unrestricted conversation, autonomous
teaching, independent world cooperation or sustained husbandry. Coarse features
can harm online choices. Confidence is uncalibrated; exposure/evidence budgets
remain finite. H4/H5, broader/longer horizons and unattended integration remain
deferred. Separate native H3 results and their authored scaffolding are described
in [the integration review](H3_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md). No consciousness, general intelligence or civilization claim is made.
