# Integrated inhabitants protocol — 23 October 2026

Registered before implementing or measuring the new integrated profile. This
adds a compatible UW10W profile; original UW9 and F0–F5 worlds and registrations
must remain unchanged. Baseline commit: 0c5098c; uncommitted F6–F12 additions
are inventoried in artifacts/f12_review_baseline_20261023.json.

## Contracts and ordering

One transaction owns perception, F0–F5 appraisal/mood/memory, F6 directional
relations, F7 public inference, F8 outcome learning, F9 private associations,
F10 evidence prerequisites, F11 acquired preferences, policy and native state.
Only each owner's permitted observations enter private updates. F0–F5 remains
the sole mood engine. Proposals enter native validation; acceptance, completion
and goals are reported separately. Authored routes and survival priorities
remain explicitly attributed. No neural training, shared lexicon, fabricated
social evidence, hidden refill or rescue is allowed.

The F9 channel uses native speech with private opaque tokens. Receivers may
associate a delivered token with their own contemporaneous bodily context or
observed material benefit. They cannot read the speaker's intended meaning.
Co-occurrence is fallible evidence, not proof of another person's emotion.

## Stage gates (bounded capability only)

- F6: all five held-out controlled directional histories prefer the reliably
  helpful partner; nonparticipants unchanged; subsequent contrary evidence
  revises the preference; replay/backup retains it. Synthetic histories test
  the mechanism and must be identified as such; real native transfer grounding
  is separately required for an integrated-runtime claim.
- F7: all five ambiguous-public-cue trials retain uncertainty, change estimates
  after disambiguating evidence, and ignore private/operator-only labels.
  A public inference must influence a feasible choice in a connected comparison.
- F8: all five real recovery probes reduce measured bodily burden through
  completed, time-costed actions. Learning records the exact acting interval.
  Learned selection must differ from frozen/disconnected in at least four of
  five context-matched choice probes. Useful urgent survival/exposure choices
  must remain protected in every probe.
- F9: native speech is completed and actually delivered; unknown partners start
  without comprehension. After six public/own-context demonstrations, acquired
  prediction accuracy >=0.8 across two balanced meanings in each seed; shuffled
  and unfamiliar controls <=0.6. Record ambiguous/misleading associations.
  No grammar or unconstrained language claim follows from this gate.
- F10: gratitude-like action requires native receipt of help plus actual own
  material benefit; connected versus disconnected behavior differs in all five
  feasible probes. No guilt/grief/envy/embarrassment runtime claim without their
  actual represented prerequisites. Report those unavailable prerequisites.
- F11: preferences and event-linked summaries survive a fresh process and
  backup; real contrasting outcomes revise a feasible choice across two contexts
  in all five seeds. Declared values and acquired preferences stay separate.
- F12: all inhabitants are scheduled with persistent cursor; 4-person runs at
  30, 90 and 180 cumulative simulated seconds, then an 8-person bounded probe,
  within wall budgets. Report survival and scarcity, actual actions/rejections,
  causal decision influences, resource ledgers, journal/state sizes and timing.
  Exact physical/cognitive replay and save/resume must pass every completed run.
  No full lifelong gate is claimed if a prerequisite stage remains unpassed.

## Verification and reporting

Use separate development and held-out saves, connected/disconnected/frozen,
relationships-disabled and shuffled-signal controls where relevant. Pure-module
checks are mechanism evidence, not natural inhabitant experiments. Native
action probes distinguish apparatus from autonomous behavior. Existing historical
gates retain their original meaning. Retain raw per-seed results and failures;
publish each gate as passed, failed or untested, never infer success from the
presence of modules. UI is an observer-only consumer and never feeds cognition.
